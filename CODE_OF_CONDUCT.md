# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in AI Council issues, pull requests, and project discussions
is expected to help create a respectful, constructive environment.

Welcome different experience levels and perspectives. Give specific feedback about
ideas and code, assume good faith, acknowledge mistakes, and respect boundaries.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention, personal attacks,
and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, issue warnings, or limit
participation when needed to protect the community. Repeated or serious misconduct
can result in a ban. Decisions should consider impact, context, and opportunities
to repair harm, with consistent treatment regardless of contribution history.

To request a private reporting channel, open an issue titled **Private conduct contact
requested** with no names, allegations, or incident details. A maintainer will arrange
a private channel before receiving the report. Where GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting is available, it can also be used to request private contact; do not post
sensitive details publicly. Share only what is needed once private contact exists.
Reports should be handled discreetly and shared only as necessary for investigation.

If a maintainer is involved, request another maintainer where possible. For conduct
that violates GitHub's own rules, use GitHub's platform reporting controls directly.
